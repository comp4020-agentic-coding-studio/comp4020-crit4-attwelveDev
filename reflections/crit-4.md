# Reflection

1. I felt that the breakthrough that carried this work forward was how easily I could let Claude handle the dependencies.
It is not uncommon for me to struggle with installing dependencies when working on these kinds of projects.
For me to completely focus on ideation and not worry about the low-level details, enabling me to quickly push out a working prototype feels like a game changer.
I have always spent too much time debugging installation errors, tinkering with the version numbers, and other small details.
The fact that I didn't even need to name a concrete dependency that would fulfil my needs really streamlines my rapid prototyping process. 

2. This being said, for larger-scale projects, I still feel the value of carefully finding the appropriate dependencies myself to ensure it truly fulfils my requirements.
Of course, I have this agentic capability readily at disposal for when I feel I can abstract away the intricate details, or when I need to rapidly push out a piece of work.
Then, it becomes a new skill in knowing and deciding when to keep working by myself, or when to allow the agent to handle the work.
